## The emoji

🤗 (U+1F917 Hugging Face)

Why: a hug is warmth made visible — the act itself, not a symbol. It is smiling too (closed happy eyes), so it carries both halves of the brief (warm AND smiling). It is the least generic warm emoji — the red heart is the most sent emoji on earth and carries no surprise; the hug says one thing clearly.
